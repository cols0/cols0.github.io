Paper Hop is a jumping game for kids and grown-ups. This page explains, in simple words, what the game keeps and where.

What do we collect?

We collect no information about you or your child: no name, email, age, location or photos. The game needs no account. The one exception is the optional ad, described under "Ads" below.

Where is progress saved?

On your device: coins, gems, outfits, best distance, missions and settings.

If iCloud is turned on for this app on your device, a copy of your progress (not the sound and language settings) is also kept in the iCloud of the Apple account you use, so it comes back when you change iPhone or reinstall the game. That copy sits in your own Apple account: it does not reach us and we cannot see it. You can turn it off in Settings, your name, iCloud, Apps.

If you delete the game, the iCloud copy stays and your progress returns when you reinstall it. To remove it for good: Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Ads

The game has optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch a short ad to earn a reward, such as continuing a run or doubling coins. No ad appears unless you tap the button. Ads are served by Google through AdMob.

• We ask Google for child-appropriate, non-personalised ads, so they are not based on your interests.

• We do not ask for tracking permission and do not track you across apps.

• To show the ad and prevent abuse, Google may process technical device information such as IP address and device type, under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy

• In Europe and the UK you see a consent message before the first ad, and you can change your choice later.

Internet

Playing needs no internet. A connection is only needed to load the optional ad. We run no tracking or analytics of our own.

Purchases

The shop has optional real-money purchases: gem packs, and the Supporter pack, which removes ads and adds a costume. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, so we never see or store your card details; we only learn which item was bought so we can add it to your game.

For parents: you can turn on Ask to Buy in Family Sharing, or block purchases in Screen Time in Settings.

Children

The game is made to be suitable for children. We collect no data from children ourselves, and ads are requested with child-appropriate, non-personalised settings.

If something changes

If a future update changes anything on this page, we will update the page and its date before the update is released.
