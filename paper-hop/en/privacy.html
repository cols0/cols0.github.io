Paper Hop is a jumping game for kids and grown-ups. This page explains, in simple words, what the game keeps and where.

What do we collect?

Nothing. The game collects no information about you or your child: no name, email, age, location or photos. It needs no account.

Where is progress saved?

Only on your device: coins, outfits, best distance, missions and settings. None of it is sent anywhere, and we cannot see it.

Deleting the game deletes the progress too.

Internet

The game does not connect to the internet. This version has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Purchases

This version has no real-money purchases. Coins and gems are only earned inside the game.

Children

The game is made to be suitable for children. Since it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

If something changes

If a future update changes anything on this page, we will update the page and its date before the update is released.
