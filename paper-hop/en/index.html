How do I play?

Tap the screen to make the courier jump, and tap again in the air for a double jump. Land in the middle of a platform to earn more coins.

Does the game need the internet?

No. You can play anywhere, even on a plane.

How do I turn off music, sounds or vibration?

On the main screen open Settings. There is a switch for each one, and the language choice too.

I changed my iPhone. Does my progress move too?

Progress is saved on the device. If you move your whole iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup, your progress may come with it. If you delete the game, the progress is deleted.
